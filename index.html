Hold Space or your finger to jump. Tap for a hop, hold longer to go higher. Press again mid-air and hold to backflip.

Tap or press Space to start

  • Space or tap: jump. Hold longer to jump higher
  • Tap again in the air and hold: backflip (+150, double flip +600)
  • Ramps and kickers launch you automatically; a JUMP! sign means jump at the lip
  • Red pads bounce you high. Tall ledges need a longer hold. Duck low branches with a short tap
  • Plank bridges collapse half a second after you touch them
  • New level every 300 m (Easy), 250 m (Medium), 200 m (Hard)
  • There are two secrets hidden somewhere
  • Coins: +25
  • Powerups: shield (saves one crash), coin magnet, super jump, double points, slow-mo, rocket (from level 2)
